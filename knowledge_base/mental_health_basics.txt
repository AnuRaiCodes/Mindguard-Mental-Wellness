MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS — STRESS, ANXIETY & DEPRESSION
=======================================================

WHAT IS STRESS?
Stress is the body's natural response to challenges or demands. A small amount of stress can be motivating, but chronic stress can harm both physical and mental health. Common sources include work pressure, academic demands, relationship conflicts, financial worries, and health concerns.

PHYSICAL SIGNS OF STRESS
• Headaches and muscle tension
• Fatigue and low energy
• Sleep disturbances (too much or too little)
• Digestive problems (nausea, upset stomach)
• Rapid heartbeat or chest tightness
• Frequent illness due to weakened immunity

EMOTIONAL SIGNS OF STRESS
• Feeling overwhelmed or out of control
• Irritability, mood swings, or short temper
• Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
• Feeling anxious or worried
• Loss of motivation or sense of enjoyment

WHAT IS ANXIETY?
Anxiety is a normal human emotion involving feelings of worry, nervousness, or unease about upcoming events. However, when anxiety becomes persistent, excessive, and interferes with daily life, it may be an anxiety disorder. Common anxiety disorders include Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD), Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder, and Specific Phobias.

SYMPTOMS OF ANXIETY DISORDERS
• Excessive, uncontrollable worry
• Restlessness or feeling on edge
• Difficulty concentrating
• Muscle tension
• Sleep problems
• Panic attacks (sudden intense fear with physical symptoms)
• Avoidance of feared situations

WHAT IS DEPRESSION?
Depression (Major Depressive Disorder) is a serious mental health condition characterized by persistent sadness, loss of interest or pleasure, and a range of emotional and physical symptoms. It is NOT a sign of weakness or a character flaw — it is a medical condition that responds to treatment.

SYMPTOMS OF DEPRESSION
• Persistent sad, empty, or hopeless feelings
• Loss of interest or pleasure in activities once enjoyed
• Changes in appetite — weight loss or gain
• Sleep disturbances (insomnia or sleeping too much)
• Fatigue or loss of energy
• Feelings of worthlessness or excessive guilt
• Difficulty thinking, concentrating, or making decisions
• Thoughts of death or suicide (seek help immediately)

IMPORTANT: These are informational descriptions. Only qualified mental health professionals can diagnose mental health conditions. If you recognize these symptoms in yourself or someone else, please consult a licensed mental health professional.

WHEN TO SEEK HELP
Seek professional help if:
• Symptoms persist for two weeks or more
• Symptoms interfere with daily functioning
• You are having thoughts of harming yourself or others
• You feel unable to cope with daily life
• Substance use is increasing as a coping mechanism

MENTAL HEALTH IN INDIA — CONTEXT
In India, mental health challenges are common but often under-discussed due to cultural stigma. According to the National Mental Health Survey, approximately 150 million Indians need mental health care. Academic pressure, family expectations, career stress, and social stigma are key contributors. It is important to normalize conversations about mental health in Indian families and communities.
