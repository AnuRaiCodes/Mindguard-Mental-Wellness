WELLNESS RESOURCES, HELPLINES & PROFESSIONAL SUPPORT
=====================================================

TYPES OF MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONALS

PSYCHIATRIST
• Medical doctor (MBBS + MD Psychiatry) who can diagnose mental health conditions and prescribe medication
• When to see: Severe depression, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, medication management

PSYCHOLOGIST (CLINICAL PSYCHOLOGIST)
• Holds an M.Phil or PhD in Clinical Psychology (RCI registered in India)
• Provides psychotherapy (talk therapy) — CBT, DBT, EMDR, etc.
• Cannot prescribe medication (in most countries)

COUNSELOR / THERAPIST
• Trained in counseling techniques
• Good for relationship issues, grief, stress, life transitions, general wellbeing

SOCIAL WORKER (MENTAL HEALTH)
• Provides community support, case management, and connects clients to resources

WHAT IS PSYCHOTHERAPY?
Psychotherapy (talk therapy) involves talking with a trained therapist to understand and work through problems. Common approaches:
• Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Identifies and changes negative thought patterns
• Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT): Teaches distress tolerance and emotional regulation
• Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT): Combines CBT with mindfulness
• Interpersonal Therapy (IPT): Focuses on relationships and communication
• Narrative Therapy: Re-authoring your life story

WORKPLACE MENTAL HEALTH
• Many organizations offer Employee Assistance Programs (EAP) — check with HR
• Workplace stress, burnout, and harassment can significantly impact mental health
• Taking regular breaks, setting boundaries, and using leave are important
• Open conversations with managers (when safe) can create supportive environments

APPS FOR MENTAL WELLNESS (Free/Low-cost)
• Wysa: AI-powered mental health app with therapist option
• MindShift CBT: For anxiety, based on CBT principles
• Headspace/Calm: Guided meditation and mindfulness
• Insight Timer: Free meditation and sleep sounds
• Sanvello: Mood tracking and coping tools

SOCIAL SUPPORT AND COMMUNITY
• Support groups for depression, anxiety, grief — search online for local groups
• Online communities (moderated, safe): Reddit r/mentalhealth, r/india mental health groups
• Religious/spiritual communities — temples, churches, mosques, gurudwaras often offer support
• NGOs: The Banyan (Chennai), Vandrevala Foundation, AASRA, iCall

BOOKS RECOMMENDED FOR MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS (India context)
• "The Palace of Illusions" — exploring emotion through Indian narrative
• "I Am the Messenger" — empathy and connection
• "Lost Connections" — by Johann Hari (understanding depression and anxiety)
• "The Mindfulness and Acceptance Workbook for Anxiety" — CBT-based
• "Man's Search for Meaning" — Viktor Frankl (purpose and resilience)

CRISIS SAFETY PLANNING
If you or someone is at risk, a safety plan includes:
1. Warning signs to watch for
2. Internal coping strategies (self-help)
3. People and settings that provide distraction
4. People to reach out to for support
5. Professionals and agencies to contact in crisis
6. Ways to make the environment safer (removing means)
