🧠 Understanding Stigma

Mental health stigma prevents people from seeking help. Remember: mental health conditions are medical issues — not character flaws or weaknesses.

💚 Therapy Works

Research shows therapy (CBT, DBT, etc.) is highly effective for anxiety, depression, and many other conditions. It's okay to ask for help.

🤝 Help a Friend

If you're worried about someone, gently ask how they are. Listen without judgment. Help them connect to professional support. Stay in touch.
